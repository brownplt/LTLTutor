Finally Review

It may be the case that you believe the {{ ltl_code('F p', operator_only=True) }} (finally) operator implicitly applies at some state, meaning it will automatically trigger at a particular point in time. However, the {{ ltl_code('F p', operator_only=True) }} operator has a different meaning in LTL. It asserts that {{ ltl_code('p') }} will eventually be true, but it doesn't specify when.
What is required is that {{ ltl_code('p') }} becomes true at least once in any future state, including potentially the present state.
