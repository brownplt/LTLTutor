Be mindful of operator precedence Tip

It may be the case that you are unsure about the precedence and associativity rules of operators in LTL formulas, and rely on parentheses alone to resolve any ambiguities. However, this is a misconception that can lead to confusion and misinterpretation of formulas. LTL follows strict rules regarding operator precedence and associativity.
In the absence of parentheses, it is important to be wary of operator precedence and associativity rules.

  • Unary operators (X, F, G, !) have the highest precedence and will bind tightly to the formula that immediately follows them.
  • Binary operators bind from tightest to loosest in the order &, |, U, ->, <->, and repeated uses of the same operator are grouped from left to right.
    For instance, an expression like a | b & c -> d would be interpreted as (a | (b & c)) -> d.
